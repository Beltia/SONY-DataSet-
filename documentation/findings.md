# Findings Template

Use this document to record final findings after running the SQL scripts and reviewing the Excel dashboard.

## Price performance

Describe the long-term closing-price trend and identify major periods of increases and declines.

## Annual performance

Identify the strongest and weakest historical years using annual return calculations.

## Volatility

Identify years and periods with elevated daily-return volatility.

## Trading activity

Discuss average volume, high-volume days and whether unusually high volume coincided with large price movements.

## Drawdowns

Identify the largest historical drawdowns from previous closing-price peaks and discuss the recovery periods.

## Moving averages

Explain what the 30-, 50- and 200-day moving averages reveal about historical trends.

## Investment characteristics

Summarise historical return, volatility, drawdowns and trading activity without presenting the findings as a prediction of future performance.

## Limitations

Explain the absence of fundamentals, dividends, macroeconomic variables, market benchmarks and other factors that could affect interpretation.
